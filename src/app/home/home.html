Business Outline.

The founder of Nicasia Holdings, Primrose Mathe, established the company in 2020 when she realized the lack of access to information young people have in terms of finding quality education, employment opportunities and how to connect with relevant organizations. With her 22 years' experience in educational recruitment with top international institutions and global corporates, she has a deep understanding of young people and the challenges they face. Nicasia Holdings is dedicated in empowering young people through skills development, training and employment leading to a country filled with potential and youth that is skilled and purpose driven.
